# Eagle AXS installation and adjustment

Follow these sections in order. Installation requires specialized tools; SRAM recommends using a qualified bicycle mechanic.

## Battery installation

Do not discard the derailleur battery block or battery cover. During transportation, remove the battery and install both pieces. Leaving the battery or derailleur terminals uncovered can damage them.

1. Insert a fully charged battery into the derailleur.
2. Close the battery latch. It should snap into place.
3. Install the battery protector until it snaps into place.
4. To remove the protector, unhook its three retention points and pull it off.

## Extension cord installation

Insert the extension cord into the derailleur and close the battery latch until it snaps into place. Follow the motor manufacturer's instructions for plug installation.

> **Warning:** The extension cord is not a battery. Never place it in a battery charger. Consult the motor manufacturer for safety instructions.

## Cassette and crankset

Use a cassette lockring tool with a guide pin. Its splines must fully engage the cassette lockring or XD locktube and must be 7–8 mm long for an XD cassette. SRAM XD cassettes fit an XD driver body or an XDR driver body with a 1.85 mm spacer.

1. Ensure the driver body and cassette interface are clean.
2. Apply grease to prevent the cassette from seizing on the driver body.
3. Seat the cassette fully with the splines engaged.
4. Apply inward pressure while tightening it with the guide-pin tool.
5. Tighten the cassette to 40 N·m (354 in-lb).

If the cassette becomes difficult to rotate after a few turns, loosen it and restart because it may be cross-threaded. Contact a SRAM dealer if lateral play remains after tightening.

Before installing the crankset or bottom bracket, consult the SRAM DUB MTB and Road Cranksets and Bottom Brackets user manual at [sram.com/service](https://www.sram.com/service).

## Rear derailleur installation

Use a rear derailleur hanger alignment tool to confirm that the hanger is straight. A bent hanger can reduce shifting performance.

Do not grease the mounting bolt or derailleur hanger threads. Push the B-adjust washer forward during installation and ensure there is no gap between it and the hanger. Tighten the mounting bolt to 11 N·m (97 in-lb).

## Chain sizing

A chain must be installed before the derailleur can be adjusted. Improper sizing or connection can cause chain failure, a crash, serious injury, or death. Consult [sram.com/service](https://www.sram.com/service) for detailed chain installation instructions.

For full-suspension frames, fully compress the rear shock before sizing the chain. Deflate or remove the shock if necessary. Frames with an upper idler pulley require a lower idler pulley for sufficient chain wrap and drivetrain performance.

### Round Eagle X-SYNC 2 chainring

1. Wrap the chain around the large chainring and largest cassette cog.
2. For full-suspension or idler-pulley bikes, add one inner and one outer link where the chain overlaps.
3. For hardtails, add two inner and two outer links where the chain overlaps.
4. Break the chain so an inner link is exposed. Discard the outer link and excess chain.

### Oval Eagle X-SYNC 2 chainring

1. Rotate the crank arm to 1 o'clock.
2. Wrap the chain around the chainring and largest cassette cog.
3. For full-suspension bikes, add one inner and one outer link at the overlap. For hardtails, add two inner and two outer links.
4. Break the chain at the inner link.

## Chain installation and PowerLock

1. Place the chain on the smallest cassette cog.
2. Rotate the derailleur cage forward and engage Cage Lock.
3. Route the chain through the derailleur pulleys and below the cage tab.

> **Warning — pinch hazard:** The spring-loaded derailleur returns rapidly from Cage Lock. Keep fingers away from pinch points.

4. Join the chain ends with a new Eagle 12-speed PowerLock. Its arrow must point in the direction of chain travel.
5. Confirm both PowerLock halves are aligned and engaged.
6. Rotate the cage forward and carefully release Cage Lock.
7. Rotate the crank until the PowerLock is above the chainstay.
8. Apply the rear brake and push the crank arm down firmly until the PowerLock clicks into place.
9. Check by hand that the PowerLock moves freely.

PowerLock is single-use. Remove it only with chain-link pliers and install a new PowerLock whenever installing a new chain.

## Chain gap

For full-suspension bikes, measure chain gap with the bike at sag. For an oval Eagle X-SYNC 2 chainring, rotate the crank arm to 4 o'clock before turning the B-adjust screw.

### With the Eagle chain-gap gauge

1. Shift to the second-largest cog (42T).
2. Snap the gauge onto the largest cog (50T or 52T), with its teeth touching the gauge surface in the window.
3. Rotate the gauge and cassette until the gauge's rear ledge contacts the upper pulley.
4. Turn the B-adjust screw until the center of the upper pulley screw aligns with the gauge pointer.
5. Keep the cassette teeth and upper pulley in contact with the gauge while adjusting, then remove the gauge.

### Without the gauge

1. Shift to the second-largest rear cog.
2. Turn the B-adjust screw until the gap between the upper pulley and tallest teeth of the largest cog is 3 mm.

## Derailleur adjustment

Shift to the second-largest cog and align the pulley wheel with its center. Hold the controller AXS button while pressing the paddle down to move the derailleur inboard or up to move it outboard. The movement may not be visible; a flashing derailleur LED confirms the command.

### Limit screws

1. Shift to the largest cog. Turn the low-limit screw until it lightly contacts the derailleur's outer link, then back it out one-quarter turn.
2. Shift to the smallest cog. Turn the high-limit screw until it lightly contacts the derailleur's inner link, then back it out one-quarter turn.

### Fine tuning

Micro-adjustments can improve shifting and eliminate rasping. Hold the controller AXS button and press the paddle down to adjust inboard or up to adjust outboard. Each adjustment is 0.2 mm.

## Controller mounting

Use only an AXS controller discrete clamp; a standard discrete clamp will not work. The manual specifies these mounting torques depending on the clamp configuration:

- Controller clamp fasteners: 2 N·m (18 in-lb) or 3 N·m (27 in-lb) as shown for the selected configuration.
- MatchMaker X clamp fastener: 5.5 N·m (49 in-lb).

Use friction paste where shown in the mounting procedure and verify the paddle can move correctly after installation.
