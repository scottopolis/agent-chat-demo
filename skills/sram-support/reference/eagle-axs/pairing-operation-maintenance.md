# Eagle AXS pairing, operation, and maintenance

## System pairing

Each AXS system has a primary component that begins and ends pairing. The Eagle AXS rear derailleur is the primary component for pairing the SRAM AXS controller. Additional AXS systems can be combined into one system by beginning pairing with any primary component.

1. At the rear derailleur, hold the AXS button until its green LED blinks slowly, then release it.
2. At the controller, hold the AXS button until its green LED blinks quickly, then release it.
3. Press and release the rear derailleur's AXS button to finish, or wait 30 seconds for pairing to time out. The green LED stops blinking.

Pairing sessions time out after 30 seconds of inactivity. Pairing does not need to be repeated after removing or replacing batteries. Repeat pairing whenever a component is replaced, added, or removed.

## Shifting

Use the SRAM AXS app to personalize system logic.

- Press the controller paddle down to move the derailleur inboard to a larger cog. Hold it for multiple shifts.
- Press the paddle up, or press the forward paddle down, to move the derailleur outboard to a smaller cog. Hold it for multiple shifts.
- Press and release the derailleur AXS button once to move outboard one position or twice quickly to move inboard one position.

AXS button functions differ by component.

## Battery indicators

The derailleur LED illuminates after a shift. Its color indicates remaining ride time. Consult the SRAM Battery and Charger user manual for charging instructions.

The derailleur LED blinks red and green when a shift is rejected. Shifts can be rejected below -15 °C.

## Controller battery replacement

Clean the controller before opening its battery cover. Use only soap, water, and a damp cloth. Rinse and allow it to dry completely.

> **Warning:** Follow the battery manufacturer's handling instructions. Keep batteries away from children, never put one in your mouth, and seek immediate medical attention if swallowed. Do not use sharp objects to remove a battery.

1. Use a coin to remove the battery cover, then remove the battery.
2. Leave the battery-cover O-ring installed to prevent moisture damage.
3. Insert a new CR2032 battery with its positive side facing into the cover.
4. Reinstall the cover and use a coin to turn it clockwise until the dots align and it locks.

## Controller paddle replacement

Install the new spring in the controller groove, compress it, and slide the paddle onto the controller. Install and tighten the pin. Move the paddle in both directions and ensure it clicks and releases each way.

The manual shows these paddle replacement torques:

- Paddle pin: 1.25 N·m (11 in-lb).
- Controller fasteners: 2 N·m (18 in-lb) and 3 N·m (27 in-lb) where shown.

## Cleaning and routine maintenance

Periodically wipe electrical components with a damp cloth. Clean only with soap and water, rinse thoroughly, let the parts dry, and then lubricate the chain.

Before cleaning, remove the derailleur battery and install the battery block. Do not use a pressure washer, acidic cleaner, grease-dissolving cleaner, chemical cleaner, or solvent. Do not soak or store electrical components in cleaner or liquid.

### Chain replacement

Measure chain wear with the SRAM Chain Wear Indicator and replace the chain at 0.8% elongation. Consult the 11 speed, Eagle, and Flattop Chain Compatible Tools compatibility map at [sram.com/service](https://www.sram.com/service) for approved tools.

### Extension cord

Leave the extension cord installed while cleaning, storing, or transporting the bike. Every 50 hours, inspect it for cuts or breaks in its insulation.

> **Warning:** Do not use a damaged extension cord and do not repair it yourself. Consult the motor manufacturer for safe handling and use an authorized dealer for repair.

## Storage and transportation

- Storage: -40 °C to 70 °C and 10% to 85% humidity.
- Operation: -10 °C to 40 °C and 0% to 100% humidity.
- Service life: five years.

Check every component's battery level before and after each use. During transportation or long-term storage, remove the derailleur battery, install the derailleur battery block, and cover the battery terminals. This prevents unintended actuation, battery depletion, and terminal damage.

## B-bolt and pulley service

Wear safety glasses when removing the B-bolt circlip. The manual's B-bolt removal procedure uses a 5 mm hex secured in a vise and two small flat-blade screwdrivers to lift the B-washer. Clean every part thoroughly before reinstallation.

For pulley replacement, orient the etched letters outward: `U` identifies the upper pulley and `L` identifies the lower pulley. Reinstall spacers with their flat sides facing outward and tighten pulley bolts to 3 N·m (27 in-lb).
